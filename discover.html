About MeetMedia

MeetMedia is a modern social networking platform designed to help people connect, meet, and build meaningful relationships. Our mission is to make networking easy, safe, and enjoyable for everyone.

Founded with the vision of bridging the gap between online and offline connections, MeetMedia combines cutting-edge technology with user-friendly design to create a unique networking experience.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is MeetMedia free to use? ▼

Yes! MeetMedia is free to use. You can create an account, discover people, send messages, and connect with others at no cost. We may offer premium features in the future, but the core platform is completely free.

How do I create an account? ▼

You can sign up with your email and password or use your Google account for quick registration. Just fill out the required information and you'll be ready to start discovering people on MeetMedia.

Is my data safe on MeetMedia? ▼

Yes, we take security very seriously. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We use Firebase's enterprise-grade security infrastructure and follow best practices for data protection. You can read our full privacy policy for more details.

Can I delete my account? ▼

Yes, you can delete your account anytime from your settings. Your profile and all associated data will be permanently removed from MeetMedia within 30 days.

How do I report inappropriate behavior? ▼

If you encounter any inappropriate behavior or harassment, you can report the user directly from their profile or message. Our support team will review the report and take appropriate action.

Can I search for people in my area? ▼

Yes! You can filter searches by location to find people near you. This makes it easy to meet people locally and attend events together.

How do messages work? ▼

Messages are sent and received in real-time through our messaging system. All messages are stored securely and you can access your full conversation history anytime.

Is there a mobile app? ▼

Our platform is fully responsive and works great on mobile browsers. We're currently developing native iOS and Android apps for an even better mobile experience.
